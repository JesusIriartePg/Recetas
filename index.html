Chicken Hot Dog

This is a very practical and easy recipe to prepare, it contains everything the body needs, proteins, carbohydrates and vegetables

Ingredients

  • Hot dog bread
  • Breaded chicken skewer
  • onion cut into chunks
  • tomato wheels
  • Lettuce
  • Tartar sauce
  • Olive oil
  • Parmesan cheese

Instructions

  1. Steaming the bread
  2. Heat the chicken skewers in olive oil
  3. Cut the vegetables into pieces
  4. Place the chicken inside the bread
  5. Add a layer of cheese
  6. Add the vegetables
  7. Add a little tartar sauce
